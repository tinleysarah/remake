FAQs

How long does coaching usually last?

Most people work with me for three to six months. That is long enough to build habits and see them hold through a few real-life disruptions.

Do I need to follow a set diet?

No. We work with the foods you like and the way your household already eats, and adjust from there.

Are sessions online or in person?

Either. Online sessions run by video call, and in-person sessions are available locally.

What happens in the free consultation?

It is a relaxed twenty-minute conversation about what you want help with and whether coaching is the right fit.

Can you work alongside my doctor?

Yes. Coaching complements medical care and never replaces it. I am happy to follow guidance from your healthcare team.

What if I need to pause?

Life happens. You can pause and pick up again when things settle, with no penalty.
