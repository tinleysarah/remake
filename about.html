My approach

Small, repeatable changes beat dramatic overhauls. We start with what already works and build from there.

Picture it

We begin by getting clear on what feeling well means to you. Not a number on a scale, but how you want your days to go: energy, sleep, mood, confidence in the kitchen.

Practise it

Then we turn that picture into a handful of habits, tried one at a time. Each session we keep what helped, drop what didn’t and choose the next small step.
