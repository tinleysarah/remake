Smashed avocado toast

A classic for good reason. Good bread, a ripe avocado and enough acid and salt to wake everything up.

Photo placeholder

Ingredients

  • 1 thick slice of sourdough or seeded bread
  • Half a ripe avocado
  • A squeeze of lime or lemon
  • A pinch of chilli flakes
  • Flaky salt and black pepper
  • 1 soft-boiled or poached egg, optional

Method

  1. Toast the bread until properly crisp.
  2. Roughly mash the avocado with the citrus juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Pile onto the toast, add the chilli flakes, and top with the egg if using.
