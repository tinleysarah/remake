Recipes · Winter · Makes 1 mask · 5 minutes

Oat and honey face mask

Not everything from the kitchen needs to be eaten. This two-minute mask is a gentle way to end a long week, using things you probably already have in the cupboard.

Photo placeholder

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons fine rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon runny honey
  • 1 tablespoon plain yogurt
  • A few drops of warm water

Method

  1. Blitz or crush the oats until they are roughly the texture of coarse flour.
  2. Stir in the honey and yogurt, loosening with warm water until spreadable.
  3. Smooth over clean skin, avoiding the eyes, and leave for ten minutes.
  4. Rinse with lukewarm water and pat dry. Patch-test first if your skin is sensitive.
