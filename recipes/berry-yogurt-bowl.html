Recipes · Summer · Serves 1 · 5 minutes

Berry yogurt breakfast bowl

A breakfast that takes less time than waiting for toast. The mix of protein, fibre and fruit keeps energy steady through the morning.

Photo placeholder

Ingredients

  • 200 g thick plain yogurt
  • A generous handful of mixed berries
  • 2 tablespoons oats or low-sugar granola
  • 1 tablespoon chopped nuts or seeds
  • A drizzle of honey, optional

Method

  1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Top with the berries, oats and nuts.
  3. Add honey if you like things sweeter, and eat straight away.
