Recipes · Autumn · Makes 4 glasses · 15 minutes plus cooling

Ginger lime fizz

A homemade alternative to shop-bought soft drinks, with far less sugar and a proper ginger kick.

Photo placeholder

Ingredients

  • A thumb-sized piece of fresh ginger, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
  • 250 ml water
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Sparkling water and ice, to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the ginger, honey and water gently for ten minutes, then leave to cool.
  2. Strain the syrup and stir in the lime juice.
  3. Pour a little syrup over ice and top up with sparkling water, adjusting to taste.
