Recipes · Spring · Serves 2 · 25 minutes

Salmon and greens grain bowl

A weeknight bowl that covers protein, whole grains and plenty of greens without much washing-up. Swap the salmon for baked tofu if you prefer.

Photo placeholder

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 150 g brown rice or farro
  • 2 large handfuls of spinach or kale
  • Half a cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to finish

Method

  1. Cook the grains according to the packet and keep warm.
  2. Season the salmon and roast at 200°C (400°F) for 12 minutes, until it flakes easily.
  3. Wilt the greens in a hot pan with a splash of water.
  4. Whisk the soy sauce, sesame oil and lemon juice into a quick dressing.
  5. Divide the grains between two bowls, top with greens, cucumber and salmon, then spoon over the dressing and scatter with sesame seeds.
